# Conduct & Expectations

Standards of behavior expected from all players, beyond the core rule list.

While the [Server Rules](server-rules.md) establish what is and is not allowed, these expectations exist to help maintain a positive roleplay environment and community culture.

## General Conduct

### Assume Good Faith

Most mistakes are not malicious.

Before escalating a situation, consider whether another player may simply be inexperienced, confused, or operating with different information than you.

Misunderstandings happen. Communication often resolves problems faster than confrontation.

### Use OOC for Out-of-Character Issues

Do not use in-character channels to argue about game mechanics, administrative actions, rule interpretations, or complaints about other players.

OOC, LOOC, and similar channels exist for out-of-character communication and clarification.

### Ahelp, Don't Retaliate

If you believe someone is breaking the rules, use **Admin Help (F1)**.

Do not take matters into your own hands.

Attempting to punish, grief, harass, or "teach a lesson" to another player because you believe they have broken the rules often creates more problems than it solves.

---

## Respect Consent

Consent is an important part of roleplay.

When someone tells you no in OOC, LOOC, or Subtle OOC, believe them.

If someone is uncomfortable with a scene, topic, or interaction, do not pressure them or attempt to convince them otherwise.

If you're unsure, ask first.

Likewise, if something makes you uncomfortable, communicate that clearly.

```admonish warning
If someone asks you to stop a roleplay interaction through OOC channels, stop.
```

---

## New Player Conduct

Veteran players are encouraged to help new players learn the game.

Everyone was new once.

Targeting, griefing, or intentionally taking advantage of inexperienced players because they are new is not acceptable.

Whenever practical, help new players learn rather than punish them for mistakes.

---

## Command Conduct

Players in command roles are held to a higher standard. Command leadership should focus on delegation, communication, and responsibility rather than personal dominance.

For full guidance, see the [Command Code of Conduct](../departments/commmand.md#command-code-of-conduct).

---

## Security Conduct

Security officers must follow [Space Law](../space-law/crimes-and-punishments.md) and the broader [Security Overview](../space-law/security-overview.md).

Security exists to enforce the law, not bypass it.

---

## Antagonist Conduct

Antagonists are expected to create enjoyable and interesting gameplay for the players around them.

The goal of an antagonist is not simply to complete objectives as quickly as possible, but to create stories, conflict, and roleplay opportunities.

Mass murder, excessive destruction, or round-ending behavior without meaningful narrative justification is not acceptable antagonist play.

For additional information, see the [Antagonist Policy](antagpolicy.md).

---

```admonish warning
"It's what my character would do" is not a valid defense for griefing, harassment, poor conduct, or rule violations.
```

## Final Note

These expectations are intended to supplement the Server Rules, not replace them.

Treat other players with respect, communicate when necessary, and remember that everyone is here to create an enjoyable roleplay experience together.
