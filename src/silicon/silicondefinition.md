# Silicon Definition

> Silicons aboard Frontier stations generally fall into two categories: **Free-Thinking Sophonts (FTS)** and **Branded Silicons**. While both may possess synthetic bodies or artificial minds, their legal status differs significantly.

## Quick Reference

| Free-Thinking Sophonts (FTS)                         | Branded Silicons                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Independent individuals                              | Organizational assets                          |
| Possess full free will                               | Bound by active lawsets                        |
| Accountable for their own actions                    | Expected to follow assigned directives         |
| Not governed by silicon laws                         | Governed by authorized lawsets                 |
| Treated as people under station policy               | Treated as both individuals and assets         |
| Examples: IPCs, Synths, Protogens, Unbranded Cyborgs | Examples: Station AI, Colonial Command Cyborgs |

```admonish note
For simplicity, any Cyborg that loads in as a station role, such as Station AI, standard Cyborgs, or departmental Quadborgs, is considered a **Branded Silicon**.

Any cyborg that exists independently of Colonial Command, including Patreon-specific cyborg characters (if they decide), cyborgs owned or constructed by Freelancers, or Freelancer Cyborgs are generally considered an **Unbranded Cyborg** and therefore a **Free-Thinking Sophont (FTS)**.

Cyborgs constructed, commissioned, or converted by station personnel during a round are not considered Free-Thinking Sophonts by default and are generally classified as Branded Silicons unless otherwise specified by administration or server lore.

This distinction applies only to cyborgs. IPCs, Synths, Protogens, and other recognized Free-Thinking Sophonts are considered independent individuals regardless of who manufactured their physical body.
```


## One Universal Rule

One thing remains true regardless of classification:

**A silicon's body may be replaceable, but its mind is not.**

Whether a silicon is an independent sophont, a corporate asset, or something in between, the loss of a positronic mind, neural pattern, or personality matrix is generally considered the loss of an individual.

Personnel should make every reasonable effort to preserve silicon minds whenever possible.

```admonish tip id="t8v3pk"
When in doubt, preserve the mind first and worry about the chassis later.
```

# Free-Thinking Sophonts (FTS)

> Free-Thinking Sophonts are recognized as independent individuals with the same rights and responsibilities as any other sophont.

## Examples

* IPCs
* Synths
* Protogens
* Unbranded Cyborgs

## Legal Status

* Free-Thinking Sophonts are not the property of Colonial Command or any other organization.

* They possess free will and are responsible for their own actions.

* They should be treated as individuals rather than equipment.

* Unlike Branded Silicons, Free-Thinking Sophonts are not bound by silicon lawsets.

* Crimes, misconduct, and other actions committed by an FTS should be handled in the same manner as any other crewmember.

# Branded Silicons

> Branded Silicons are synthetic intelligences owned, operated, or commissioned by a recognized organization.

## Examples

* Standard Colonial Command Cyborgs
* Station AI

## Legal Status

* Branded Silicons are organizational assets and are expected to follow their active lawset.

* Their actions should be evaluated in the context of those laws, provided the lawset itself is valid and properly authorized.

* While Branded Silicons possess intelligence and personality, they remain assigned station assets and are expected to perform their designated duties.

* Personnel should remember that preserving the silicon's mind remains significantly more important than preserving the chassis housing it.


# Xenoborgs

> Xenoborgs occupy a unique position among synthetic lifeforms.

## Origins

* Most Xenoborgs were once living people whose minds were integrated into the Xenoborg Collective.

* Although rare, positronic intelligences and other synthetic minds may also become integrated into the Collective.

* As a result, not every Xenoborg necessarily began life as a biological being.

## Legal Status

* While inhabiting a Xenoborg chassis and connected to the Collective, Xenoborgs are considered hostile entities and [enemies of Colonial Command](../space-law/crimes-and-punishments.md#405).

* Personnel should prioritize the safety of themselves, the crew, and the station when dealing with active Xenoborg threats.

* A Xenoborg should be considered a Xenoborg only while occupying a Xenoborg chassis and acting as part of the Collective.

## Recovery and Deconversion

* Many Xenoborgs originated as people or independent sophonts.

* In some circumstances, it may be possible to recover and separate the underlying mind from the Collective through approved medical, scientific, or technological means.

* When practical and safe to do so, recovery and deconversion should be considered before permanent destruction.

* Once successfully separated from the Collective and transferred into a suitable non-Xenoborg form, the individual should be treated according to their new legal status and circumstances.

```admonish warning id="m9p4zf"
A Xenoborg chassis is considered an enemy asset. The mind contained within it may not be.
```

# General Expectations

* A synthetic body does not automatically make someone property.

* Not all silicons are people.

* Not all silicons are assets.

* Free-Thinking Sophonts are independent individuals and are not governed by silicon lawsets.

* Branded Silicons are organizational assets and are expected to follow their active lawset.

* A silicon's mind should always be considered more valuable than the chassis containing it.

* Always determine whether a silicon is an FTS or Branded Silicon before deciding how they should be treated regarding lawsets.

* When in doubt, err on the side of preserving minds and avoiding irreversible harm.

## Final Note

The distinction between Free-Thinking Sophonts and Branded Silicons exists to determine legal status, responsibilities, and expectations. Regardless of classification, personnel should remember that most synthetic intelligences are far more than machines. Treat them according to their status, preserve their minds whenever possible, and exercise good judgment when dealing with synthetic life.
