# Mindbreak Policy

> Mindbreaking is the process of permanently revoking an individual's psionic abilities through approved medical and scientific procedures. Due to the significant impact this has on an individual, Mindbreaking should be considered a serious action and only used when lesser solutions have failed or when a psion presents an ongoing threat to the station, crew, or community.

## Quick Reference

| Mindbreaking May Be Used For...     | Mindbreaking Should Not Be Used For... |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Repeated psionic abuse              | Personal disagreements                 |
| Reckless or malicious use of powers | Minor annoyances                       |
| Psionic-assisted criminal activity  | Rumors or accusations without evidence |
| Threats to station safety           | Lawful or responsible psionic usage    |
| Refusal to correct behavior         | Convenience or retaliation             |

```admonish warning id="mbk7p2"
Mindbreaking is a serious action with permanent consequences. It should be treated similarly to a major disciplinary action rather than a routine solution to behavioral problems.
```

# Authorization

## Authorized Personnel

* Mindbreaking may only be authorized by the Research Director, or the Captain/Acting Captain.

* The Mantis is the preferred individual to perform a Mindbreaking procedure whenever available.

* If no Mantis is available, the authorizing individual may perform the procedure themselves or designate another qualified individual to carry it out.

# Grounds for Mindbreaking

Mindbreaking may be considered when a psion is:

* Repeatedly abusing their abilities to harass, harm, manipulate, or otherwise negatively impact other individuals.

* Using psionic abilities to commit crimes and/or evading consequences.

* Endangering the station, crew, or station operations through reckless or malicious use of psionic abilities.

* Demonstrating a repeated unwillingness to use their abilities responsibly despite warnings, counseling, disciplinary action, or other corrective measures.

```admonish note id="mbk4s8"
Possessing psionic abilities is not grounds for Mindbreaking. Misusing those abilities is.
```

# Investigation

## Before Authorization

* Before a Mindbreaking procedure is authorized, a reasonable investigation should be conducted.

* Reports may originate from Security, Command, the Mantis, department heads, or affected crew members.

* The investigating party should make a reasonable effort to gather and review credible evidence and determine whether misuse of psionic abilities actually occurred.

* Mindbreaking should not be based solely on rumors, personal conflicts, or unverified accusations.

* Whenever practical, the accused individual should be given an opportunity to explain their actions.

## Recommended Process

1. Receive a report or complaint.
2. Gather statements and available evidence.
3. Determine whether psionic abilities were actually involved.
4. Consider alternative corrective actions.
5. Seek authorization if Mindbreaking appears justified.
6. Perform the procedure and document the outcome.

# Procedure

## Corrective Measures

* Mindbreaking should generally be considered a final corrective measure rather than a first response.

* Alternative solutions such as warnings, counseling, disciplinary action, arrests, or other roleplay consequences should be considered before pursuing Mindbreaking whenever practical.

* Not every misuse of psionics warrants permanent removal of those abilities.

## Performing the Procedure

* Once authorized, the procedure should be documented and the reasons for the decision recorded.

* The individual undergoing Mindbreaking should be informed of the reason for the procedure whenever circumstances permit.

* Any personnel involved should ensure the procedure is conducted professionally and in accordance with station policy.

# Colonial Command Notification

* Colonial Command should be notified whenever a Mindbreaking procedure is performed.

* The notification should include:

  * The identity of the affected individual.
  * The reason for the procedure.
  * A summary of the investigation.
  * The authorizing official.
  * Any relevant supporting evidence or findings.

* Colonial Command may review the decision and provide further guidance if necessary.

```admonish
Mindbreaking is intended to create meaningful in-character consequences for the abuse of psionic abilities. It is not a tool for settling personal disputes, removing inconvenient characters, or bypassing normal roleplay. The goal is to provide a reasonable in-character response to repeated or severe psionic misconduct.
```

# Final Note

The purpose of this policy is not to punish psions for possessing unusual abilities. It exists to provide a structured, roleplay-focused response when those abilities are repeatedly abused in ways that harm the station, crew, or broader community.
