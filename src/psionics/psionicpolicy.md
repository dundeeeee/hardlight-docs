# Psionic Policy

> Psionic abilities are a fairly normal part of life within Frontier space. Players who select the Psionic trait gain access to telepathic communication with other psions and may develop additional psionic abilities. These abilities are intended to create interesting character interactions, unique situations, and memorable roleplay.

## Quick Reference

| Good Psionic Play                    | Poor Psionic Play                  |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Creates roleplay                     | Creates disruption                 |
| Uses powers with purpose             | Uses powers solely because you can |
| Builds character interactions        | Uses powers to annoy others        |
| Treats powers as part of a character | Treats powers as a toy             |
| Accepts consequences                 | Expects immunity from consequences |

## General Expectations

* Psionic abilities are intended to enhance roleplay, not replace it.

* Powers should generally be used in ways that make sense for your character, their personality, and the situation at hand.

* Psionic abilities should contribute to the story being told rather than exist solely to create disruption.

* Having a psionic ability does not require you to constantly use it, nor does possessing a power automatically justify using it in every situation.

## Telepathy

* Telepathic communication is a normal psionic ability and may be used freely by psions.

* Psions are encouraged to use telepathy to build relationships, coordinate with one another, share information, and create roleplay opportunities.

* Telepathy is simply another form of communication available to psions and should be treated accordingly.

## Responsible Use

* Not every use of a psionic power requires extensive justification. Psionic abilities are a normal part of a psion's life.

* However, significant, dangerous, or highly disruptive uses of psionic abilities should generally have an in-character reason behind them.

* Consider how your actions affect the people around you and whether those actions create meaningful roleplay opportunities.

* The goal is not to avoid using your powers. The goal is to use them in a way that contributes positively to the round.

## Abuse of Psionic Powers

Examples of psionic abuse include:

* Repeatedly using powers to harass, annoy, or target other players.

* Using powers solely to create disruption without meaningful roleplay.

* Using powers recklessly in ways that repeatedly endanger the station or crew without reasonable justification.

* Treating psionic abilities as a tool for trolling rather than roleplay.

* Ignoring repeated warnings, counseling, or corrective measures regarding irresponsible psionic behavior.

## Mantis and Mindbreaking

* The Mantis serves as the station's primary advisor regarding psionic matters.

* Psions are encouraged to seek guidance from the Mantis regarding unusual powers, uncontrolled abilities, or psionic concerns.

* Repeated or severe abuse of psionic powers may result in investigation under the [Mindbreak Policy](../mindbreak-policy/mindbreak.md).

```admonish note
Psionic powers are not the problem. Abuse of those powers is. The purpose of this policy is not to restrict psions, but to ensure psionic abilities remain a source of interesting roleplay rather than unnecessary disruption.
```

## Final Note

Psionic abilities are intended to create interesting stories, unique character interactions, and unusual situations. Use your powers to contribute to the round, develop your character, and create memorable roleplay for those around you.
