# Service

> Service is responsible for maintaining crew morale, quality of life, and many of the station's day-to-day comforts.

## Service Roles

| Role              | Responsibility                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Head of Personnel](headofpersonnel.md)| Oversees the Service Department, manages civilian personnel, handles access and job changes, and assists with station administration.        |
| Chef              | Prepares meals, feeds the crew, and ensures the station has access to quality food throughout the shift.                                     |
| Botanist          | Grows crops, produces ingredients, maintains hydroponics, and supplies the Chef and station with food, plants, and other botanical products. |
| Bartender         | Serves drinks, operates the bar, and provides a social gathering place for crew members to relax and socialize.                              |
| Janitor           | Keeps the station clean, removes hazards, and ensures public areas remain safe and presentable.                                              |
| Service Worker    | Assists the Service Department wherever needed and supports other service roles as required.                                                 |
| Chaplain          | Provides spiritual guidance, moral support, and religious services to those who seek them.                                                   |
| Station Host      | Entertains the crew through broadcasts, interviews, announcements, and other public programs.                                                |
| Librarian         | Maintains the library, preserves knowledge, and provides educational, cultural, and recreational opportunities for the crew.                 |
| Valet             | Assists visitors and crew members with transportation, hospitality, and general customer service throughout the station.                     |
| Boxer             | Participates in and promotes recreational sporting activities, providing entertainment through friendly competition.                         |
| Clown             | Entertains the crew through humor, harmless mischief, and lighthearted interactions that help improve station morale.                        |
| Mime              | Entertains the crew through silent performance, visual storytelling, and creative roleplay interactions.                                     |
| Musician          | Provides musical entertainment and contributes to the station's atmosphere through artistic expression.                                      |


### Department Expectations

* Service personnel should strive to create enjoyable roleplay and social interactions for the crew.

* Service personnel all contribute to the station in different ways. No service role should be viewed as inherently more important than another.

* Service personnel are encouraged to host events, create activities, and foster social interaction among the crew.

* Service staff should remain mindful of ongoing emergencies and station conditions. While social activities are important, there is a time and place for them.

* Harmless pranks, entertainment, and lighthearted interactions are encouraged when appropriate to the situation and server rules.

* Service personnel are often some of the most visible members of the station and should help create an engaging environment for everyone around them.

* While Service may not directly maintain the station's infrastructure or security, maintaining crew morale is a valuable contribution in its own right.

#### Final Note

The station is more than power generation, medical equipment, and security patrols. Service personnel help make it feel alive, and their contribution to the round is just as important as any other department.
