# Medical

> Medical is responsible for the health, wellbeing, and mental welfare of station personnel.

## Medical Roles

| Position              | Purpose                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Chief Medical Officer](chiefmedicalofficer.md) | Oversees the Medical Department, coordinates patient care, manages medical personnel, and ensures proper medical procedures are followed.    |
| Paramedic             | Responds to medical emergencies, retrieves injured personnel, provides emergency treatment, and transports patients to Medical.              |
| Geneticist            | Studies genetics, performs genetic modifications and treatments, and assists in the diagnosis and correction of genetic abnormalities.       |
| Chemist               | Manufactures medicines, medical compounds, and other approved chemicals required for patient care and station operations.                    |
| Medical Doctor        | Diagnoses illnesses and injuries, treats patients, performs routine medical care, and helps maintain the overall health of the crew.         |
| Medical Intern        | Assists Medical personnel with patient care and departmental duties while learning the responsibilities of the Medical Department.           |
| Psychologist          | Provides mental health support, counseling, and emotional care to crew members experiencing stress, trauma, or other psychological concerns. |

### Department Expectations

* Medical personnel are expected to provide care to those who need it regardless of personal feelings, departmental affiliation, or social status.

* Patients should be treated with professionalism, respect, and dignity whenever possible.

* Medical staff should make reasonable efforts to preserve life and minimize unnecessary harm.

* Confidential medical information should remain confidential unless disclosure is required for safety, legal, or operational reasons.

* Chemists, Doctors, Psychologists, and Paramedics should cooperate to provide effective care rather than operating as entirely separate groups.

* Medical is encouraged to roleplay injuries, treatment, recovery, mental health, and long-term care rather than reducing every patient interaction to mechanical treatment.

#### Final Note

Medical exists to preserve life and care for the crew. Professionalism, compassion, and cooperation are just as important as medical knowledge.
